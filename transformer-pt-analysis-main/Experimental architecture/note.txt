### Research Notes: Attention-Head Capacity and Grokking

Preliminary experimental observations suggest that attention-head capacity may influence the generalization behavior of neural networks trained on modular addition tasks.

When the attention-head capacity is relatively low, the model exhibits conventional training behavior, with training and validation accuracy improving at approximately the same rate.

However, as attention-head capacity increases, the model tends to exhibit grokking, where training accuracy reaches nearly 100% significantly earlier than validation accuracy. After additional training, validation accuracy improves rapidly, eventually approaching 100%.

**Hypothesis:** Increasing attention-head capacity may encourage initial memorization of training examples, resulting in delayed generalization. Conversely, lower-capacity attention heads may encourage earlier learning of generalizable mathematical representations.

Further experiments are required to determine whether this behavior is consistent across different model configurations, random seeds, and modular arithmetic tasks.
